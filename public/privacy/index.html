1. Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the use of Prep App For 7 Graders ("CHSB Prep", "the Application"), created and published by Ibrohimbek Xujanazarov ("we", "us", or "our"). The Application is an interactive educational examination preparation platform designed for 7th-grade students preparing for Choraklik Summativ Baholash (ChSB) tests.

We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our users, particularly young learners and students. This document outlines what data is collected, how it is handled, and your rights regarding your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. The Application collects only the data necessary to provide personalized test results and progress tracking:

  • User Profile Information: A nickname or display name chosen by the user and an optional student ID for school leaderboard identification. Real names, phone numbers, home addresses, and payment details are NEVER required or collected.
  • Academic Performance Data: Answers submitted during practice tests, percentage scores, time taken, earned milestone badges, and unlocked cosmetic rank titles/frames.
  • Local Application Settings: User preferences including sound effects toggle, high-contrast display mode, and font scale stored locally on the user's device via standard browser LocalStorage.

3. Camera & Hardware Permissions

Camera Proctoring Simulation: The Application includes an optional student practice proctoring simulator feature. When granted permission by the user, the camera feed is processed strictly and entirely on the user's local device.

NO video footage, photos, or biometric data are ever recorded, saved, stored, or transmitted to any server or third party. You can disable camera access at any time through your browser or device settings without affecting standard test functionality.

4. Cloud Storage & Third-Party Services

To enable cross-device progress synchronization and class leaderboards, the Application integrates the following trusted third-party service:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Authentication): Used to securely store test scores, unlocked badges, and leaderboard rankings. Firebase operates under Google's enterprise-grade security and privacy compliance standards.

We do not use any third-party behavioral advertising, data brokers, tracking cookies, or commercial analytics SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because the Application is intended for 7th-grade students (typically ages 12–14), protecting children's privacy is of paramount importance:

  • We do not solicit or knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental or school guidance.
  • We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade student data with any advertisers or third parties.
  • The application contains no advertisements, in-app purchases, or behavioral tracking mechanisms.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Practice exam history and leaderboard scores are retained solely for the user's active learning session. Users or parents may request immediate deletion of their leaderboard entry or score history at any time by contacting us at the email provided below.
